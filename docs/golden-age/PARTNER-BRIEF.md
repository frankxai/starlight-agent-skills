# A practice companion around SOMA — proposal for review

Draft only. No outreach has been sent. No agreement or endorsement is implied.

## The proposal

Give students a useful bridge between sessions: arrive with an intention, follow their
authorized SOMA practice, return comfortably, and turn a chosen insight into one ordinary
act. An optional private memory helps them revisit their own themes and follow-through.

The working demonstration contains three portable skills, original Starlight visualization
scripts, preparation/return cards, and a tested action/memory preview. It runs as a
conversational companion without requiring a new app or an instructor dashboard.

## Division of responsibility

SOMA would own approval of its teaching, instructors, brand, licensed content and method
updates. Starlight would provide companion design, technical packaging, versioning and
evaluation. Students retain ownership of their reflections. Instructor access is explicit
and limited to selected material; no raw-journal feed is part of the default product.

## What to demonstrate

1. A student opens their actual authorized session and receives a brief preparation card.
2. After return, they choose one detail to retain; AI does not interpret it for them.
3. They choose an action and receive a small useful draft.
4. They inspect a memory proposal, decline it or approve exact selected content.
5. The next review asks what actually happened.

The first demo can use a synthetic garden-workshop example. The original Starlight
visualization is optional; it does not replace or reproduce SOMA's Golden Age exercise.

## Pilot ask

Invite a small instructor review, then consider a two-week usability pilot with three
instructors and 12–20 consenting adults. Assess student usefulness, actual follow-through,
instructor time and any privacy/agency failures. Do not frame this as a clinical trial
or promise better health results. The pilot design is a proposal, not a scheduled event.
