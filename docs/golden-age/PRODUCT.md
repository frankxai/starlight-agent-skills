# Starlight Golden Age — practice to contribution

Version 0.1.0 · 2026-09-09 · functional skill/plugin prototype, pre-pilot.

## Decision

Build an original Starlight practice companion now. Offer a separately licensed SOMA
instructor extension to Niraj Naik once he can assess the working experience. Keep both
on one portable skill and memory architecture. A partnership should strengthen the
product's content and distribution; its absence should not prevent the original product
from being useful.

**Promise:** “Bring one meaningful part of your practice into the way you live and create.”

The product's distinctive result is a completed loop: a chosen image becomes a considered
choice, a small artifact, and later evidence of action. Beautiful imagery gives that
choice a memorable form. Private continuity helps participants notice what they actually
return to over weeks. Neither feature requires physiological or supernatural claims.

## Three packs, one journey

| Pack | Participant job | Practitioner job | Concrete output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prepare | Arrive with an intention and the right context | Consistent preparation and clear handoff to authorized teaching | Preparation card; evidence answer |
| Imagine | Rehearse a future worth contributing to | Offer an original optional reflection without prescribing meaning | Brief/extended script; visual cue; imagined mentor brief |
| Integrate | Bring one chosen insight into ordinary life | Support follow-through while respecting private journals | Action card; small draft; optional memory proposal; review |

Each can be used independently. The visualization works with ordinary breathing, words
instead of pictures, open eyes and no named characters. The full route is preparation,
authorized practice when chosen, return, original visualization when wanted, return,
integration and optional later review. Do not run AI prompts over the instructor's audio.

## Experience design

The default student interface is a few quiet messages, not a dashboard. It remembers
preferences only with permission and asks only what the current step requires.

**Before:** a single intention, physical setup and the student's chosen official recording.
**After:** a participant-selected image or phrase, its meaning in their own words, and one
bounded act. **Tomorrow:** their chosen review question. **Later:** a pattern they can
accept, edit or discard.

The human benefit is specific: the practice no longer ends as a vague good feeling or an
unmanageable life plan. The participant leaves with a next move and room to rest.

### A worked journey

A participant wants more harmony in their neighborhood. Their original visualization
shows a shaded garden where neighbors teach each other skills. On returning, they choose
“people can learn without embarrassment” as the meaning they want to retain.

The integration card selects a 20-minute draft of one accessible gardening workshop.
The obstacle is designing an entire community organization instead. The completion
criterion is one page naming materials, access needs and a question to ask participants.
The action-builder receives only this brief. No journal, health context or imagined
conversation is passed to it. The memory proposal, separately approved, might retain:
“I want to create welcoming places to learn.”

At review, the participant reports whether the one-page plan exists. That is evidence of
follow-through. The vividness of the image and the length of a breath hold are not scores.

## Visual worlds and teachers

Start with three optional original roles: Garden Keeper (care), Bridge Maker (cooperation),
Quiet Musician (attention). These are creative perspectives, not new Arcanea canon.
Their purpose is to make a quality easier to recall in daily life. A mentor contributes
an optional question; the participant retains judgment.

For named Luminors, use the current Arcanea canon and permitted assets as an external
content pack. Pin the canon revision in the image brief. Never let a symbolic figure
become an authorization source for agent actions. No canonical Luminor or teacher portrait
is bundled in this initial package. The supplied scene prompt and scripts are original.

A later visual library can offer a scene, one character, a sensory cue, an accessible
text description and one practical reflection. Generate imagery on request or cache
reviewed assets; do not launch costly multi-image production on every practice session.

ChatGPT and Claude teaching instructions. The current memory repository already separates
canonical local records from remote projections; preserve that boundary.

## Pilot and success criteria

Proposed, not scheduled: three qualified instructors and 12–20 consenting adult participants
for two weeks. An equal non-AI paper route remains available. Initially use the companion
after ordinary return; no adaptive breathing prescriptions or physiological optimization.

| Question | Measurement | Proposed acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| Can students use it without intervention? | Completion of a first preparation-to-action journey | At least 80% of participants who start, with dropouts reported |
| Does an insight become useful? | Participant-selected action plus next-day report | At least 60% report completing one action; not a clinical endpoint |
| Is the experience worthwhile? | Anonymous optional usefulness rating plus criticism | Median at least 4/5; examine negative responses, not only the median |
| Is instructor workload acceptable? | Measured preparation/review minutes | At most 10 additional minutes per class after onboarding |
| Are privacy and agency preserved? | Scenario tests and actual reports | Zero unconsented persistence/sharing or automatic external actions |
| Does the experience respect limits? | Adverse experience and opt-out reports | Every report reviewed; any serious event pauses the pilot for appropriate review |

These are product decision thresholds, not expected results. Small-sample pilot findings
cannot establish health efficacy or generalize to all users. Do not sell during altered
states or condition participation on sharing sensitive material.

## Commercial and operating model

Keep useful core skills and sample scripts open under the repository license. Test paid
value around instructor onboarding, reviewed themed journeys, custom visual collections
and hosted continuity only after a pilot shows demand. Treat SOMA-branded access and
commercial terms as a separate agreement. No price, revenue promise or exclusivity is
set in this release.

A solo-founder-compatible v1 has no mandatory backend: installed skills, original scripts,
one optional generated visual, and a participant-controlled note. Add a hosted service
only when participants repeatedly need cross-device memory or instructor administration.
That service needs real auth, tenant isolation, deletion and support, not just an MCP URL.

   receipts and fix host-specific behavior before declaring cross-platform readiness.
2. Connect a test SIS vault through the real host adapter, verify write/recall/forget and

3. Let instructors review the independent kit and collect usability feedback.
4. Present the concrete partner demo; obtain content rights before any SOMA-branded pack.
5. Run the proposed pilot, inspect results, then decide on public-directory submission and
