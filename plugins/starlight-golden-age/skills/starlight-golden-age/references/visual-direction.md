# Visual direction and imagined teachers

## Original scene language
Use lived beauty: warm morning light, believable materials, ecological abundance, skilled work and humane public places. Blend a small amount of luminous fantasy into recognizable life. Avoid pyramids of power, guru thrones, medical imagery, miracle claims, overwhelming cosmic eyes and obligatory religious symbols.

Default creative brief:
- Purpose: a contemplative visual cue, not a depiction of a predicted event.
- Place: a waterside garden and open workshop within a cared-for city.
- Human activity: people planting, teaching, repairing or making music.
- Composition: an accessible path into the image, quiet foreground, clear focal subject.
- Light: pearl daylight, leaf green, restrained gold; believable surfaces.
- Accessibility: no strobe, no rapid animation; optional static image and plain alt text.
- Privacy: no identifying faces or private life details unless selected for this image.

## Optional original mentor roles
These roles are creative devices, not new Arcanea canon or real beings.

| Role | Embodies | Useful question | Ordinary-world translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Garden Keeper | Care and patience | What needs tending regularly? | One repeatable act of care |
| The Bridge Maker | Cooperation and repair | Whose perspective is missing? | A conversation or accessible design |
| The Quiet Musician | Attention and beauty | What deserves more careful listening? | Listen, rehearse, record or make space |

Keep the figure at a participant-selected distance; allow changing or removing it. Do not say the figure knows the participant's destiny, diagnoses an illness or speaks for God. Respect the participant's faith without validating unverifiable supernatural claims.
