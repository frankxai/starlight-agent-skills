# Media Studio

## Purpose

Create distinctive media with durable editable masters, controlled generation spend, complete rights provenance and channel-ready renditions.

## When it fires

- Producing original songs, scores, albums, art packs, motion graphics, videos, sound packs or licensed media collections.
- Enriching a product or world with governed music, visual, motion or video assets.
- Preparing Bandcamp, distributor, BeatStars, Canva, Creative Market, Gumroad, itch.io or owned-store packages.

## Inputs

- Product manifest, Media Studio task envelope, creative brief and exact source revision.
- Pinned brand/design pack, approved reference registry, asset IDs, rights/consent/split records, external credit ceiling and target channels.
- Prior masters, continuity rules, release metadata and known restrictions.

## Workflow

1. Resolve the product, Media Studio pack, media policy, design authority and channel adapters. Distinguish canon, references, generated layers, factual UI/text and approved owned assets.
2. Bind emotional premise, audience effect, medium, constraints, exclusions, token budget and external credit/cash ceiling. Do not spend without an explicit envelope.
3. Assign creative direction, production, continuity, rights and independent technical verification to distinct owners.
4. Preflight references, consent, samples, contributor splits, inbound licenses, model/tool terms, attribution and prohibited uses before irreversible production.
5. Produce editable source and masters. Composite typography, logos, factual data and partner marks deterministically from exact approved assets rather than asking generative tools to imitate them.
6. Run taste, originality, continuity, similarity, brand, audio/visual technical, accessibility, caption, checksum and rendition inspection.
7. Compile channel-specific masters, previews, metadata, licenses and instructions while preserving one work identity and rights record.
8. Emit an evidence receipt with prompts/tools where relevant, contributors, versions, spend, QC, approvals, placements, takedown and remaining human decisions.

## Output contract

Return creative premise, editable masters, approved renditions, asset/cue sheet, rights and provenance record, channel packages, spend actuals, technical/taste evidence, release receipt and release decision.

## Tools and MCP

Use GitHub for specifications, policy, manifests and receipts; Drive for private masters and review handoff; Figma/Canva for governed deterministic layouts; Remotion or HyperFrames for code-driven motion; specialist audio/video tools for production and mastering; and runtime storage only through the accepted media-fabric contract.

## Quality bar

- The work has an authored premise and survives a substitution test.
- Every asset and contributor is traceable; samples, voices, marks and likenesses are controlled.
- Editable masters, delivery masters, previews and metadata agree.
- Human sensory review complements technical checks.
- Publication, paid generation beyond the envelope, licensing grants and minting remain explicit human gates.
