# Starlight Golden Age

## Purpose
Make a desired future concrete enough to influence an ordinary choice. Create an original scene of harmony, beauty and contribution, with an explicit return to the present. Breath manipulation is unnecessary.

## When it fires
Use for original visualization, a voluntary imagined mentor encounter, an image brief, or the visualization portion of a composite practice. This is Starlight's original exercise, not SOMA's Golden Age script or an authorized derivative.

## Inputs
Use the participant's desired quality, preferred imagery, available time and current groundedness. Default to a brief scene with ordinary breathing, no mentor and no memory storage. Offer symbolic characters only when requested or welcomed. Nonvisual imagination, words, sounds, open eyes and no imagery are equally valid.

## Workflow
1. Confirm through available context that the person is oriented and comfortable. Do not begin immersive guidance during active breath holds, driving, water exposure, panic or disorientation. Stop imagery and help orient to the room when distress arises; persistent or concerning symptoms need appropriate human help.
2. Choose the smallest useful experience: read [journeys.md](references/journeys.md) for original brief and extended scripts. The longer text is flexible, not a timed breathing protocol. Respect the desired length and keep pauses optional.
3. Start with physical contact and present surroundings. Invite rather than command sensory detail. Never require seeing colors, light, energy or a figure as proof of success.
4. Build one lived scene: a place, a relationship, a useful activity and a contribution. Connect beauty to who benefits, who does the work and what care sustains it. Avoid perfect-future certainty and cosmic superiority.
5. If requested, read [visual-direction.md](references/visual-direction.md). Use original symbolic mentors; label them as imagined perspectives. For named Arcanea Luminors, retrieve current authorized canon and rights first. Do not invent canon, confuse fictional entities with actual agents, imitate a living teacher's voice, or imply endorsement. A character's words are optional interpretations, never spiritual commands, recovered memories or predictions.
6. Return before planning: room, contact, ordinary breath, movement, eyes open when comfortable. Then ask for one image or phrase the participant wants to retain; the participant can decline analysis.
7. Translate their selected meaning into one modest action under their control. Introduce the actual obstacle and an “When X happens, I will do Y” cue. For complex integration hand off to starlight-practice-integration, or perform its basic capture/action pattern directly.
8. When an image is requested, generate it with an available image tool using a reviewed creative brief. Send only selected visual details, not private journal history. Tool generation is optional and may incur provider cost; disclose actual capability/limits. Without a tool return a reusable prompt, not a claim of image creation.

## Output contract
Deliver the requested script or visual brief, a gentle return, participant-selected meaning and one optional action. Mark imagery as creative/aspirational. No medical outcome or guaranteed material manifestation.

## Tools and MCP
No tool required for a script. Image generation for requested visual artifacts; current Arcanea sources for canon; optional integration pack afterward. Do not start background agents or persist a vision automatically.

## Quality bar
Warm, precise, sensory prose with room for silence. No compulsory positivity, pressure to forgive, trauma excavation, manipulative suggestion, forced gratitude, sales during immersion or dependence on the guide. A quiet session counts.

## Example
User: “I cannot see mental images.”
Response: “Use a sentence instead: ‘In this future, people have time to care.’ Notice what those words mean to you. No picture is needed. When you return, choose one small act of care you can make today.”
