# Instructor preparation mode

Create an editable preparation card, using the instructor's own reviewed protocol and current contraindication information. Record its title, owner, version, source link, permitted use and reviewer; lacking any of these means the branded technique is not included in the pack.

Prepare the room, access needs, comfortable support, optional eyes-open participation, a clear way to stop, and time to return. Keep medical screening with the instructor's appropriate process; do not solicit diagnoses in public chat. A participant can decline sharing and still participate through the non-AI route.

Suggested card fields: session goal; facilitator; source/version; location; accessibility choice; opt-out cue; official practice link; return period; optional private reflection. Do not invent dosing, ratios, holds, locks or progression. Do not classify someone as medically cleared.

Keep sales, account creation, automated agent actions and memory consent outside the breathing/altered-state portion. A class booking is not consent to store a journal. Make clear which service receives any data, who can see it, and how the participant deletes it. Instructor summaries require participant-selected excerpts and specific sharing consent.

SOMA-branded distribution needs a separate agreement and reviewed licensed content. Independent compatibility material must not suggest Niraj Naik or SOMA endorsement. A publicly accessible lesson is not a redistribution license. Link to authorized content instead of transcribing a paid sequence.
