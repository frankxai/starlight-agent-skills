# Liquid glass identity

Overlapping sheets represent specialist studios gathered into one release.

512-unit geometry; 110-unit tile radius; restrained specular rim; #FFC19A accent. Light/dark renditions share geometry. PNG listing logos are 1024 square; composer icons are 128 square. Transparent space outside the rounded tile preserves the host surface. Readability is checked at 32 and 64 pixels. Exact symbols carry meaning; glass is material treatment, not evidence of execution. SVG is self-contained and deterministic.
