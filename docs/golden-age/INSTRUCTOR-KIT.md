# Instructor kit — independent companion prototype

Use alongside the instructor's authorized class. The kit contains no SOMA breathing
ratios, holds, music, Golden Age transcript or teacher impersonation. Replace the
session-specific blank fields with reviewed information before participant use.

## Before the class

**Your session:** title/link ______; facilitator ______; date ______.
**Content record:** owner ______; version ______; permitted use ______; reviewer ______.

“Choose one quality you would like to bring into today. You can keep that intention
private. Settle securely and comfortably. Follow the authorized instructor's guidance,
and let them know privately about relevant screening concerns through their usual
process. You can pause or stop. Eyes-open participation and a non-AI route are available.
We will leave time at the end to return before any reflection.”

**Facilitator checks:** accessible space/support; no water, driving or machinery;
opt-out signal explained; screening process complete within professional scope;
return time protected. Do not substitute an AI checklist for observation or screening.

## Return card

“Notice your surroundings and the support beneath you. Let breathing return to its
ordinary rhythm. Move gently when comfortable. There is nothing you have to describe.
When ready, you can keep a word, a picture, a line on paper—or simply rest.”

**Optional private reflection**

- A detail I choose to retain: ______
- What it means to me, provisionally: ______
- One small act I choose: ______
- When ______ happens, I will ______ for up to ______ minutes.
- My likely obstacle: ______. Done means: ______.

Offer the original Starlight visualization only as an optional separate exercise once
participants are comfortable and oriented. Never layer it over retention to distract
from the urge to breathe.

## Sharing and memory card

Default: no instructor journal collection and no extra persistent write by the companion.
The host's chat-history settings still apply when someone uses a hosted assistant.

“I choose to share/save exactly: ______. Destination/recipient: ______. Purpose: ______.
Visibility: ______. Retention: ______. Deletion route: ______.”

Record this choice only outside the active practice and after return. A person can decline
without losing access to the session. Show the exact text before it is saved or shared.
A checkbox does not replace an understandable choice. Do not bundle research, marketing
and instructor-sharing consent together.

## Next-day review draft

“Did the action you chose happen? What helped, what got in the way, and what would you
like to keep or change?”

Send or schedule only on the participant's requested channel with actual authorization.
This document has not scheduled or sent a message.

## Instructor feedback form

Capture extra minutes spent, unclear instructions, where the student needed help,
usefulness rating, opt-outs and any concerning experiences. Record the denominator
and missing responses. Exclude names and raw journals from aggregate product reports.
Small-group comments may still identify people; review them before sharing.
